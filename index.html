About me

Engineering, data and geospatial insights

I’m Ivana Figueiredo Bezerra, an Aerospace Engineering graduate from the University of Brasília (UnB), Brazil, with experience in geospatial data analysis, GIS and Python-based process automation.

From April 2024 to December 2025, I worked as a Geospatial Data Intern at Brazil’s National Electric Energy Agency (ANEEL). My work included maintaining geospatial datasets, processing raster and vector data, working with satellite imagery, preparing maps in QGIS and ArcGIS, and developing Python scripts to automate workflows.

This portfolio showcases projects in GIS, remote sensing, spatial databases and data visualization, from data preparation and geospatial analysis to cartographic products and the communication of results.
